Start with the upstream implementations and evaluation guides used in the [Quickstart](/#quickstart) and the worked examples below.

## Software and evaluation guides

- [TabPFN](https://github.com/PriorLabs/TabPFN) — Implementation, checkpoint access, and estimator documentation.
- [TabICL](https://github.com/soda-inria/tabicl) — An additional implementation of in-context learning for tables.
- [scikit-learn · cross-validation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html) — Split design for grouped observations and independent test sets.

## Worked method examples

These reference walkthroughs use simulated data to illustrate study design. They are outside the product catalog. Each includes a downloadable notebook and links to its sources.

- [Evaluating Domain Adaptation on Tabular Data](/examples/domain/) — Compare source-only, target-only, and pooled training under a change in site.
- [Split Conformal Intervals with TabPFN](/examples/statistics/) — Keep fitting, calibration, and coverage assessment on separate rows.
- [Testing Generalization to New Experimental Batches](/examples/science/) — Hold out entire batches and compare prediction errors with a median baseline.
